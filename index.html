FAQs

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers based on our current service information.

Where are you located?

We are based in Naic, Cavite and may accommodate nearby areas depending on technician availability and schedule.

What are your operating hours?

Our regular service hours are Monday–Sunday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Do you offer same-day service?

Same-day service may be available depending on technician availability, location, and urgency.

Can I request a quote first?

Yes. You may request an estimate. For accurate pricing, we may ask for photos, videos, service details, or an actual site inspection.

Are the prices on the page fixed?

Prices are generally starting prices unless specifically identified as fixed. Final pricing may depend on the actual condition and requirements.

Is there an additional charge for locations that are far away?

Possibly. Customers outside the regular service area may have an additional travel or transportation fee, which will be explained before confirmation.

Do I need an inspection first?

For simple services, not always. Repairs, major plumbing work, termite treatment, and complicated aircon problems may require inspection before a final quotation.

Can I send a photo or video?

Yes. Photos and videos are highly recommended because they can help us understand the concern before scheduling.

Do I need to know the exact problem before booking?

No. You can simply describe what you are experiencing. The technician can assess the actual problem during inspection.

Do you accept emergency service requests?

Yes, emergency service requests may be accommodated during operating hours depending on technician availability and location.

What should I do if there is a major water leak?

If possible and safe, turn off the nearest water valve or main water supply and keep electrical equipment away from water.

What should I do if my aircon smells like it is burning?

Turn off the aircon immediately. If safe, disconnect the power supply. Do not continue using the unit until it has been inspected.

How much does aircon cleaning cost?

Window-type cleaning starts around ₱700; split-type cleaning starts around ₱1,200; deep or chemical cleaning is approximately ₱1,000–₱2,500+. Final price depends on the unit and service required.

How much does plumbing service cost?

Plumbing assessment or minor service starts around ₱350–₱500. Actual repair costs depend on the problem, labor, and materials.

How much does pest control cost?

General pest treatment may start around ₱1,500. Termite and larger infestations may require inspection and a customized quotation.
